TONY Voice Finder

Last updated: October 2026

What TONY does

TONY is an Android utility designed to help you locate a nearby phone using a voice wake name and device responses.

Microphone and voice processing

The current app design uses the microphone continuously so it can hear the configured wake name. The current app's How It Works screen states that voice recognition is processed on the device.

Permissions

TONY may request permissions needed for microphone listening, vibration, camera/flashlight functionality, notifications and related Android functionality.

Background operation

Android manufacturers may apply different battery and background restrictions. TONY provides guidance for keeping the voice finder active.
